# 1000 Basic English Words A1–A2 with Audio

Build your beginner English vocabulary with 1000 basic English words. These Anki flashcards use short English definitions and audio to help you recall everyday vocabulary at A1–A2.

![Anki: apple](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/antkmorn/lingvoberry-learning-resources/master/assets/basic1000/en.png)

1000 words and 1000 cards: 600 A1 + 400 A2. Family, food, home, work, travel, time and common everyday words. Each answer includes a short definition, American English IPA and built-in audio.

**apple → A round fruit, often red or green, that grows on a tree** · /ˈæpəl/ · audio

Import the .apkg file into Anki. Start with 5–10 new words a day. Say the meaning before revealing the answer; listen, repeat and rate your recall. Cards and audio work offline after import.

This is a curated beginner selection, not an exact frequency ranking or a complete A1–A2 syllabus. One short meaning is shown per word; context may change the meaning. Audio is a synthetic American English voice, not a human recording.
