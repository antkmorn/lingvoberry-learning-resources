# English Arguments 60

Evidence, caveats and clear conclusions. A free deck of **60 expressions in context** for upper-intermediate and advanced english learners.

![Front and back of an actual card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/antkmorn/lingvoberry-learning-resources/master/assets/arguments60-preview.png)

## Practise the whole expression

Read the meaning and complete the gap in the sentence. First-letter hints narrow down the target expression. Turn the card to check the answer, the complete example and a usage note. Then make a sentence of your own.

These are self-review cards: another expression may fit a sentence, but the card practises the named expression. Start with 5–10 new cards a day and review the cards Anki schedules.

## Inside the deck

- Evidence and reasoning
- Qualification and uncertainty
- Contrast and concession
- Causes and consequences
- Scope and comparison
- Evaluation and conclusions

Each expression has one recall card. English-only text, with no audio or translations. The intended learner range is a practical guide, not a certified CEFR assessment.

## Example

**Recall:** A change in the sample is ［…］ for the difference.

**Answer:** a plausible explanation

**Meaning:** an account that could reasonably be true.

**Usage:** Plausible means believable, not confirmed.

## Download and use

- [Anki deck, editable CSV and complete ZIP](https://github.com/antkmorn/lingvoberry-learning-resources/releases/tag/arguments60-v1)
- Import the `.apkg` in Anki, AnkiDroid or AnkiMobile. Desktop Anki and AnkiDroid are free; AnkiMobile is a paid iOS app.
- The UTF-8 CSV contains `phrase`, `meaning`, `prompt`, `example`, `usage`, `topic`, `hint` and `source_url`. Read or edit it in a spreadsheet. For an Anki text import, map the fields to the matching note type or use the prepared APKG.

The cards work offline after import. The optional LingvoBerry link needs an internet connection.
